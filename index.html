Entry
While drawing, type values, Tab for the next field, Enter to confirm
Sense
A projection typed without sign takes the sense pointed by the mouse. “-” forces the negative sense (West/PS, South/AFT, down)
⌫
While drawing with empty fields: undo the last segment
Esc
Clear the fields, then finish the line
Offshore
Ship frame: North → FWD, South → AFT, East → SB (starboard), West → PS (portside). Bearing 0° = FWD, 90° = SB
Angles
Horizontal plane: angle from the transverse axis (E-W or PS-SB). Vertical planes: slope from horizontal
MTO
Flanges, gaskets and studs are derived from flanged valves, flange joints, spectacle blinds and blind flanges. Bolting per ASME B16.5 (classes 150 to 2500); stud length depends on facing and gasket
Welds
Counted at BW fitting ends, WN flanges, unions and branch outlets; inch-dia = Σ NPS per weld
